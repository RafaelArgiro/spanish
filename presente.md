# El Presente de Indicativo

The present tense in Spanish. This explainer goes from fully regular verbs, through the
predictable patterns (stem changes and spelling changes), to the twelve verbs that have
to be memorised outright, and finishes with reflexive verbs.

---

## 0. What the present tense is used for

The Spanish present covers more ground than the English simple present.

- **Habitual actions** — *Trabajo desde casa.* (I work from home.)
- **Actions happening right now** — *¿Qué haces? Leo.* (What are you doing? I'm reading.)
  Spanish does not need the progressive here the way English does.
- **Near future** — *Mañana salgo a las ocho.* (Tomorrow I leave at eight.)
- **Ongoing situations with a time expression** — *Hace tres años que vivo aquí.*
  (I have lived here for three years.) English uses the perfect; Spanish uses the present.
- **Narrating past events vividly** (historical present) — *En 1492 Colón llega a América.*

### The six persons

| Person | Pronoun | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st singular | yo | |
| 2nd singular | tú | informal |
| 3rd singular | él / ella / usted | *usted* is formal "you" but takes the 3rd person form |
| 1st plural | nosotros / nosotras | |
| 2nd plural | vosotros / vosotras | Spain only |
| 3rd plural | ellos / ellas / ustedes | in Latin America *ustedes* replaces *vosotros* entirely |

Subject pronouns are usually dropped, because the ending already tells you who the
subject is. *Hablo* can only mean "I speak".

## 1. Regular verbs

Every Spanish infinitive ends in **-ar**, **-er**, or **-ir**. To conjugate a regular
verb you remove those last two letters to get the **stem** — `hablar` → `habl-`,
`comer` → `com-`, `abrir` → `abr-` — then add the ending for the person you want.

### The three sets of endings

| Person | -AR *hablar* (to speak) | -ER *comer* (to eat) | -IR *abrir* (to open) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| yo | habl**o** | com**o** | abr**o** |
| tú | habl**as** | com**es** | abr**es** |
| él / ella / usted | habl**a** | com**e** | abr**e** |
| nosotros | habl**amos** | com**emos** | abr**imos** |
| vosotros | habl**áis** | com**éis** | abr**ís** |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | habl**an** | com**en** | abr**en** |

Notice that **-er** and **-ir** verbs are identical except in the *nosotros* and
*vosotros* forms. Those two forms are the only place the -er/-ir distinction shows up in
the present tense.

*Hablo español con mi familia.* — I speak Spanish with my family.
*Comemos a la una.* — We eat at one.
*Abren la tienda a las nueve.* — They open the shop at nine.

### Points worth fixing in memory

- The **yo** form always ends in **-o** for regular verbs, no matter the group.
- The **vosotros** form always carries a written accent: *habláis, coméis, abrís*.
- The *nosotros* form of **-ar** and **-ir** verbs (*hablamos, abrimos*) is identical to
  the preterite form. Context tells you which one is meant.

## 2. Stem-changing verbs

In these verbs the **endings stay completely regular**. What changes is the last vowel of
the stem, and only when that vowel is stressed.

The stress falls on the stem in four of the six forms: *yo, tú, él, ellos*. It falls on
the ending in *nosotros* and *vosotros*. So the change happens in four forms and not in
the other two.

If you lay the six forms out in a column, the changed forms make an L-shape around the
unchanged middle. This is why they are often called **boot verbs** or **shoe verbs**.

```
yo        CHANGED  ┐
tú        CHANGED  │
él/ella   CHANGED  │
nosotros  regular  ┘
vosotros  regular
ellos     CHANGED
```

A stem change is never predictable from the infinitive alone. Dictionaries mark it, for
example `pensar (ie)` or `dormir (ue)`.

### 2.1 E → IE

The most common stem change. The stressed **e** of the stem becomes **ie**.

**PENSAR — to think**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | p**ie**nso |
| tú | p**ie**nsas |
| él / ella / usted | p**ie**nsa |
| nosotros | pensamos |
| vosotros | pensáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | p**ie**nsan |

Other verbs in this group:

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| querer | quiero | to want / to love |
| empezar | empiezo | to begin |
| comenzar | comienzo | to begin |
| entender | entiendo | to understand |
| perder | pierdo | to lose |
| cerrar | cierro | to close |
| sentir | siento | to feel |
| preferir | prefiero | to prefer |
| despertar | despierto | to wake |
| recomendar | recomiendo | to recommend |
| mentir | miento | to lie |
| nevar | nieva | to snow (3rd person only) |

One verb goes further: **adquirir** changes **i → ie** (*adquiero*), and so does
**jugar**, covered separately below.

### 2.2 Accent additions

A small group of verbs ending in **-iar** and **-uar** do not change their vowel, but
they do move the stress onto it. That stress has to be written as an accent, which breaks
the vowel away from the one next to it so it becomes its own syllable.

**ENVIAR — to send** (i → í)

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | env**í**o |
| tú | env**í**as |
| él / ella / usted | env**í**a |
| nosotros | enviamos |
| vosotros | enviáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | env**í**an |

**CONTINUAR — to continue** (u → ú)

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | contin**ú**o |
| tú | contin**ú**as |
| él / ella / usted | contin**ú**a |
| nosotros | continuamos |
| vosotros | continuáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | contin**ú**an |

Same L-shaped pattern as the vowel changes: four forms accented, *nosotros* and
*vosotros* not.

| i → í | u → ú |
| --- | --- |
| enviar → envío | continuar → continúo |
| confiar → confío | actuar → actúo |
| guiar → guío | graduar → gradúo |
| variar → varío | situar → sitúo |
| esquiar → esquío | evaluar → evalúo |
| espiar → espío | acentuar → acentúo |

Also in this family: **reunir** → *reúno*, **prohibir** → *prohíbo*, **aislar** → *aíslo*.

**Important:** most **-iar** verbs are simply regular and take no accent. Compare:

- *estudiar* → **estudio**, estudias, estudia (no accent)
- *cambiar* → **cambio**, cambias, cambia (no accent)
- *enviar* → **envío**, envías, envía (accent)

There is no rule to tell these apart. They have to be learned individually.

### 2.3 O → UE

The stressed **o** of the stem becomes **ue**.

**PODER — to be able to / can**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | p**ue**do |
| tú | p**ue**des |
| él / ella / usted | p**ue**de |
| nosotros | podemos |
| vosotros | podéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | p**ue**den |

Other verbs in this group:

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| volver | vuelvo | to return |
| dormir | duermo | to sleep |
| contar | cuento | to count / to tell |
| encontrar | encuentro | to find |
| recordar | recuerdo | to remember |
| mostrar | muestro | to show |
| costar | cuesta | to cost (3rd person only) |
| morir | muero | to die |
| probar | pruebo | to try / to taste |
| almorzar | almuerzo | to have lunch |
| mover | muevo | to move |
| resolver | resuelvo | to solve |
| soler | suelo | to usually do |
| llover | llueve | to rain (3rd person only) |

### 2.4 E → I

The stressed **e** of the stem becomes **i**. This change happens **only in -ir verbs**.

**PEDIR — to ask for / to order**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | p**i**do |
| tú | p**i**des |
| él / ella / usted | p**i**de |
| nosotros | pedimos |
| vosotros | pedís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | p**i**den |

Other verbs in this group:

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| servir | sirvo | to serve |
| repetir | repito | to repeat |
| vestir | visto | to dress |
| medir | mido | to measure |
| despedir | despido | to dismiss / to see off |
| competir | compito | to compete |
| impedir | impido | to prevent |
| freír | frío | to fry |
| reír | río | to laugh |
| sonreír | sonrío | to smile |

*reír* and *sonreír* combine the vowel change with a written accent: *río, ríes, ríe,
reímos, reís, ríen*.

Two very common verbs, **seguir** and **decir**, belong here too but also have an
irregular *yo* form. They are covered in sections 3.5 and 4.

### 2.5 U → UE

Exactly one verb in the language does this.

**JUGAR — to play**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | j**ue**go |
| tú | j**ue**gas |
| él / ella / usted | j**ue**ga |
| nosotros | jugamos |
| vosotros | jugáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | j**ue**gan |

*Juego al fútbol los sábados.* — I play football on Saturdays.

### 2.6 Adding Y (-uir verbs)

Verbs ending in **-uir** insert a **y** between the stem and the ending, in the same four
boot forms. This exists so the *u* and the following vowel do not collapse into one
sound.

**CONSTRUIR — to build**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | constru**y**o |
| tú | constru**y**es |
| él / ella / usted | constru**y**e |
| nosotros | construimos |
| vosotros | construís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | constru**y**en |

Other verbs in this group:

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| incluir | incluyo | to include |
| destruir | destruyo | to destroy |
| huir | huyo | to flee |
| concluir | concluyo | to conclude |
| influir | influyo | to influence |
| contribuir | contribuyo | to contribute |
| distribuir | distribuyo | to distribute |
| sustituir | sustituyo | to substitute |
| atribuir | atribuyo | to attribute |

**Watch out:** verbs ending in **-guir** (*seguir*, *distinguir*) are **not** in this
group. The *u* there is silent and only exists to keep the *g* hard. See section 3.5.

## 3. Irregular YO forms

These verbs are regular in five of the six forms. Only the **yo** form is irregular, and
it is irregular for a reason: either the spelling has to change to keep the pronunciation
consistent before the **-o** ending, or the verb simply takes an extra letter.

If a verb also has a stem change, that stem change still applies on top.

### 3.1 C → ZC

Verbs ending in **-cer** or **-cir** where the letter before the *c* is a **vowel**.

**CONOCER — to know (a person or place)**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | cono**zc**o |
| tú | conoces |
| él / ella / usted | conoce |
| nosotros | conocemos |
| vosotros | conocéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | conocen |

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| parecer | parezco | to seem |
| ofrecer | ofrezco | to offer |
| agradecer | agradezco | to thank |
| aparecer | aparezco | to appear |
| crecer | crezco | to grow |
| nacer | nazco | to be born |
| obedecer | obedezco | to obey |
| pertenecer | pertenezco | to belong |
| merecer | merezco | to deserve |
| reconocer | reconozco | to recognise |
| conducir | conduzco | to drive |
| traducir | traduzco | to translate |
| producir | produzco | to produce |
| introducir | introduzco | to introduce / to insert |

This is a large and productive group. If you meet a new verb ending in a vowel plus
*-cer* or *-cir*, assume **-zco**.

### 3.2 C → Z

Verbs ending in **-cer** or **-cir** where the letter before the *c* is a **consonant**.

The *c* in *vencer* is soft because it sits before *e*. Before the *o* of the yo ending it
would turn hard, so it is written **z** instead, which keeps the soft sound.

**VENCER — to defeat / to expire**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | ven**z**o |
| tú | vences |
| él / ella / usted | vence |
| nosotros | vencemos |
| vosotros | vencéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | vencen |

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| convencer | convenzo | to convince |
| ejercer | ejerzo | to practise (a profession) |
| esparcir | esparzo | to scatter |
| torcer | tuerzo | to twist (also o → ue) |
| cocer | cuezo | to boil / to cook (also o → ue) |

### 3.3 G → J

Verbs ending in **-ger** or **-gir**. Same logic as above: the *g* is soft before *e* and
*i*, so before the *o* of the yo ending it is written **j** to keep that sound.

**COGER — to take / to catch**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | co**j**o |
| tú | coges |
| él / ella / usted | coge |
| nosotros | cogemos |
| vosotros | cogéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | cogen |

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| escoger | escojo | to choose |
| recoger | recojo | to pick up |
| proteger | protejo | to protect |
| dirigir | dirijo | to direct / to manage |
| exigir | exijo | to demand |
| fingir | finjo | to pretend |
| elegir | elijo | to choose (also e → i) |
| corregir | corrijo | to correct (also e → i) |

### 3.4 Adding G

A group of common verbs simply inserts a **g** in the yo form. There is no pronunciation
reason for it; it has to be memorised. These are often called **-go verbs**.

**SALIR — to leave / to go out**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | sal**g**o |
| tú | sales |
| él / ella / usted | sale |
| nosotros | salimos |
| vosotros | salís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | salen |

| Verb | yo form | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| poner | pongo | also *suponer* → supongo, *proponer* → propongo |
| valer | valgo | |
| hacer | hago | the *c* is replaced by *g* |
| traer | traigo | inserts **ig** |
| caer | caigo | inserts **ig** |
| tener | tengo | also stem-changing e → ie in other forms |
| venir | vengo | also stem-changing e → ie in other forms |
| decir | digo | also stem-changing e → i in other forms |
| oír | oigo | also adds **y** in other forms |

The first five (*poner, valer, hacer, traer, caer*) are regular everywhere except the yo
form. The last four are fully irregular and appear again in section 4.

### 3.5 -GUIR verbs

In **-guir** verbs the *u* is silent. It is only there to keep the *g* hard before *e* and
*i*. Before the *o* of the yo ending the *g* is already hard, so the *u* is dropped.

**SEGUIR — to follow / to continue** (also stem-changing e → i)

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | si**g**o |
| tú | sigues |
| él / ella / usted | sigue |
| nosotros | seguimos |
| vosotros | seguís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | siguen |

| Verb | yo form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| conseguir | consigo | to get / to obtain |
| perseguir | persigo | to chase / to pursue |
| distinguir | distingo | to distinguish (no stem change) |
| extinguir | extingo | to extinguish (no stem change) |

*distinguir* and *extinguir* show the spelling rule on its own: the only change is the
dropped *u*.

### 3.6 Other: SABER and CABER

Two verbs have a yo form that fits no pattern at all.

**SABER — to know (facts, information, how to do something)**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | **sé** |
| tú | sabes |
| él / ella / usted | sabe |
| nosotros | sabemos |
| vosotros | sabéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | saben |

*sé* carries a written accent to distinguish it from the pronoun *se*.

**CABER — to fit**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | **quepo** |
| tú | cabes |
| él / ella / usted | cabe |
| nosotros | cabemos |
| vosotros | cabéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | caben |

Two more verbs belong in this "yo form only" category even though they are usually listed
as fully irregular: **ver** → *veo* and **dar** → *doy*. Both are otherwise regular.

## 4. Fully irregular verbs

These twelve change in more than just the yo form. They are also the most frequently used
verbs in the language, so the effort of memorising them pays off immediately.

### SER — to be (identity, origin, essential qualities)

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | soy |
| tú | eres |
| él / ella / usted | es |
| nosotros | somos |
| vosotros | sois |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | son |

*Soy de Bélgica. Ella es ingeniera.*

### ESTAR — to be (location, temporary states)

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | estoy |
| tú | estás |
| él / ella / usted | está |
| nosotros | estamos |
| vosotros | estáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | están |

Note the written accents on every form except *yo* and *nosotros*.

*Estoy cansado. El libro está en la mesa.*

### TENER — to have

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | tengo |
| tú | tienes |
| él / ella / usted | tiene |
| nosotros | tenemos |
| vosotros | tenéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | tienen |

Irregular yo form (*-go*) plus the e → ie stem change. The same pattern applies to
*mantener*, *obtener*, *contener*, *detener*.

*Tengo treinta años.* — I am thirty years old. Spanish uses *tener* for age, hunger,
thirst, cold, fear and sleepiness, where English uses "to be".

### OÍR — to hear

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | oigo |
| tú | oyes |
| él / ella / usted | oye |
| nosotros | oímos |
| vosotros | oís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | oyen |

Irregular yo form (*-igo*), **y** inserted in the boot forms, and a written accent on
*oímos* and *oís* to keep the *o* and *i* in separate syllables.

### PONER — to put / to place

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | pongo |
| tú | pones |
| él / ella / usted | pone |
| nosotros | ponemos |
| vosotros | ponéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | ponen |

Only the yo form is irregular. The same pattern applies to *suponer*, *proponer*,
*componer*, *imponer*.

### HABER — to have (auxiliary) / there is, there are

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | he |
| tú | has |
| él / ella / usted | ha |
| nosotros | hemos |
| vosotros | habéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | han |

*haber* is almost never used on its own. It has two jobs:

1. **Auxiliary for the perfect tense**, always followed by a past participle —
   *He comido. ¿Has visto la película?*
2. **Impersonal "there is / there are"**, in the special form **hay** — *Hay un problema.
   Hay tres sillas.* The form *hay* never changes for number.

### QUERER — to want / to love

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | quiero |
| tú | quieres |
| él / ella / usted | quiere |
| nosotros | queremos |
| vosotros | queréis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | quieren |

In the present tense this is a plain e → ie boot verb. Its irregularity shows up in other
tenses (*quise*, *querré*).

### HACER — to do / to make

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | hago |
| tú | haces |
| él / ella / usted | hace |
| nosotros | hacemos |
| vosotros | hacéis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | hacen |

Only the yo form is irregular. *hacer* is also used for weather: *Hace frío. Hace sol.*

### IR — to go

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | voy |
| tú | vas |
| él / ella / usted | va |
| nosotros | vamos |
| vosotros | vais |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | van |

The present forms come from a different Latin verb than the infinitive, which is why they
share no letters with *ir*.

**ir + a + infinitive** is the standard way to express the near future:
*Voy a estudiar esta noche.* — I am going to study tonight.

### VER — to see

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | veo |
| tú | ves |
| él / ella / usted | ve |
| nosotros | vemos |
| vosotros | veis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | ven |

The stem keeps the *e* from the old infinitive *veer*, which is why the yo form is *veo*
and not *vo*. Note that *veis* has no accent, unlike most *vosotros* forms.

### DECIR — to say / to tell

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | digo |
| tú | dices |
| él / ella / usted | dice |
| nosotros | decimos |
| vosotros | decís |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | dicen |

Irregular yo form (*-go*) plus the e → i stem change.

### DAR — to give

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | doy |
| tú | das |
| él / ella / usted | da |
| nosotros | damos |
| vosotros | dais |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | dan |

Regular everywhere except *doy*. Like *veis*, the form *dais* takes no accent.

### The four verbs ending in -OY

Only four verbs in the entire language have a yo form ending in **-oy**:

s**oy** · est**oy** · v**oy** · d**oy**

## 5. Reflexive verbs

A reflexive verb is one where the subject and the object are the same person. The
infinitive is marked with **-se** on the end: *lavarse*, *levantarse*, *llamarse*.

### The reflexive pronouns

| Person | Pronoun |
| --- | --- |
| yo | me |
| tú | te |
| él / ella / usted | se |
| nosotros | nos |
| vosotros | os |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | se |

### How to conjugate

Two steps:

1. Drop the **-se** and conjugate the verb exactly as you normally would, applying any
   stem change or irregular yo form.
2. Put the matching reflexive pronoun **before** the conjugated verb.

**LLAMARSE — to be called**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | **me** llamo |
| tú | **te** llamas |
| él / ella / usted | **se** llama |
| nosotros | **nos** llamamos |
| vosotros | **os** llamáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | **se** llaman |

*¿Cómo te llamas? Me llamo Ana.*

**LEVANTARSE — to get up**

| Person | Form |
| --- | --- |
| yo | me levanto |
| tú | te levantas |
| él / ella / usted | se levanta |
| nosotros | nos levantamos |
| vosotros | os levantáis |
| ellos / ellas / ustedes | se levantan |

### Stem changes still apply

Making a verb reflexive changes nothing about the conjugation itself.

| Infinitive | Pattern | yo form |
| --- | --- | --- |
| despertarse | e → ie | me despierto |
| sentarse | e → ie | me siento |
| acostarse | o → ue | me acuesto |
| dormirse | o → ue | me duermo |
| vestirse | e → i | me visto |
| despedirse | e → i | me despido |
| ponerse | irregular yo | me pongo |
| irse | fully irregular | me voy |

Two of these collide in the yo form and have to be told apart by context:

- *me siento* from **sentarse** — I sit down
- *me siento* from **sentirse** — I feel

*Me siento en la silla porque no me siento bien.* — I sit down on the chair because I do
not feel well.

### Where the pronoun goes

| Structure | Rule | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conjugated verb | Pronoun before the verb | **Me** ducho por la noche. |
| Negative | Pronoun stays with the verb, *no* goes first | **No me** ducho por la mañana. |
| Infinitive | Attach to the end, or put before the first verb | Voy a ducharme. / **Me** voy a duchar. |
| Gerund | Attach to the end, or put before the first verb | Estoy duchándo**me**. / **Me** estoy duchando. |
| Affirmative command | Must attach to the end | Dúcha**te**. |
| Negative command | Must go before the verb | No **te** duches. |

When a pronoun is attached to a gerund or a command, the stress of the original word does
not move, so a written accent is added to preserve it: *duchándome*, *dúchate*,
*levántate*, *siéntese*.

### Body parts and clothing take the definite article

Where English uses a possessive, Spanish uses *el / la / los / las*. The reflexive pronoun
already tells you whose it is.

- *Me lavo **las** manos.* — I wash **my** hands.
- *Se quita **el** abrigo.* — He takes off **his** coat.
- *Nos cepillamos **los** dientes.* — We brush **our** teeth.

### The reflexive pronoun can also be reciprocal

In the plural, *nos* and *se* can mean "each other" instead of "oneself".

- *Nos vemos mañana.* — We'll see each other tomorrow.
- *Se escriben todas las semanas.* — They write to each other every week.

### The reflexive form can change the meaning

For many verbs, adding the pronoun is not just a grammatical detail; it produces a
different verb.

| Plain | Reflexive |
| --- | --- |
| ir — to go | irse — to leave / to go away |
| dormir — to sleep | dormirse — to fall asleep |
| acordar — to agree | acordarse — to remember |
| llevar — to carry | llevarse — to get along / to take away |
| poner — to put | ponerse — to put on / to become |
| volver — to return | volverse — to become |
| parecer — to seem | parecerse — to look alike |
| quedar — to be left | quedarse — to stay |
| dar — to give | darse cuenta — to realise |

*Duermo ocho horas* means I sleep eight hours. *Me duermo a las once* means I fall asleep
at eleven.

Some verbs only exist in the reflexive form: *arrepentirse* (to regret), *atreverse* (to
dare), *quejarse* (to complain), *suicidarse*.

## 6. Summary

| Category | What changes | Which forms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | nothing | — |
| Stem change e → ie, o → ue, e → i, u → ue | stem vowel | all except nosotros / vosotros |
| Accent addition (-iar, -uar) | written accent on stem vowel | all except nosotros / vosotros |
| Adding y (-uir) | **y** inserted | all except nosotros / vosotros |
| c → zc, c → z, g → j, adding g, -guir | spelling or extra letter | yo only |

| Reflexive | pronoun added | all six |

The endings themselves are never irregular, with the single exception of the twelve verbs
in section 4. Everything else is a change to the stem.
